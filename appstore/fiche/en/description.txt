SBC Network puts the whole Sniper Business Center directory on your phone. Find the right member in seconds, reach them on WhatsApp, and keep your address book up to date automatically.

SEARCH THE DIRECTORY
• Search members by name or profession.
• Filter by country, region, profession and age range.
• Sort results by confidence score.

A FULL PROFILE FOR EVERY MEMBER
• Profession, city, country and photo.
• A confidence score and reviews from other members.
• Leave your own review of members you have worked with.

CONTACT IN ONE TAP
• Open WhatsApp with a message already written.
• Save a member to your phone's contacts, without duplicates.

SMART SYNC
• Create a criteria (for example "Designers in Douala") in three steps.
• Review the matching members: tick, untick, then save everyone in one go.
• Get an alert when a new member matches your criteria.
• Follow your syncs from the Sync dashboard.

AND MORE
• Keep your favourite members in Favourites.
• See which members saved you to their address book.

SBC Network is for Sniper Business Center members only. You sign in with your SBC account.

Contacts access is used only to save the members you choose and to avoid duplicates. Your address book is never sent to our servers.
