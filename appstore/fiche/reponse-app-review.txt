GUIDELINE 2.1(b) - BUSINESS MODEL

1. Who are the users that will use the paid subscriptions in the app?
Members of Sniper Business Center (SBC), an open business network of entrepreneurs, freelancers and small business owners in French-speaking Africa (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Benin…). Each membership is bought by an individual for their own business activity.

2. Where can users purchase the subscriptions that can be accessed in the app?
Only from SBC, on its website (sniperbuisnesscenter.com), outside the app. The app doesn't sell memberships, show their prices, or link to a page where they are bought.

3. What specific types of previously purchased subscriptions can a user access in the app?
The SBC membership, in two tiers: CLASSIQUE and CIBLE. The membership is a broader service than this app. It includes business trainings and courses, in-person events and networking meetups, the member tools on the SBC website, and a referral programme. SBC Network is one of the ways members use that membership on their phone. After sign-in, the app reads whether the account has an active membership, and which tier.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?
An active SBC membership unlocks the SBC member directory and the features built on it:
- searching members (CLASSIQUE: filter by country; CIBLE: filter by region, profession, age and interests),
- contacting members on WhatsApp and saving them to the phone's contacts,
- contact sync by criteria, and alerts when a new member matches.
Everything else is free and needs no membership. That includes creating an SBC Network account in the app, posting a request for a service and comparing the professionals who answer, setting up a professional profile and receiving client requests, and "Augmenter mes vues WhatsApp". In this build, receiving client requests is free in the iOS app, and the app no longer shows any price or tells users to pay for anything outside the app. There are no in-app purchases.

5. Are the enterprise services in your app sold to single users, consumers, or for family use?
To single users. SBC memberships are bought by individual entrepreneurs and professionals for their own use. They aren't sold to companies for their employees, and there are no family plans.
