GUIDELINE 2.1 - AUTHORISATION CODE
Users never need an authorisation code. The "J'ai un code d'autorisation" button was a leftover from an older sign-in flow, where the code had to be pasted in by hand. Sign-in now returns to the app automatically, and we've removed the button from this build.

GUIDELINE 3.2 - BUSINESS
SBC Network is intended for a general, public audience:

1. Is the app restricted to users who are part of a single company or organization?
No. Sniper Business Center (SBC) is an open business network, not a company with employees or contractors. Anyone can download the app and use it.

2. Is the app designed for use by a limited or specific group of companies or organizations?
No. It isn't built for any particular company. Anyone can create an account, and any professional or business can become an SBC member.

3. What features are intended for use by the general public?
Anyone can create an SBC Network account in the app, with no invitation or pre-approved registration. With that account, they can post a request for a service and compare the professionals who answer, set up their own professional profile and receive requests, and use "Augmenter mes vues WhatsApp" (grow the views of their WhatsApp status). The member directory, contact sync and new-member alerts list the SBC network's members, so these features are for SBC members.

4. How do users obtain an account?
In the app, by tapping "Créer un compte SBC Network" on the first screen (name, country, phone number and password). Anyone who wants to join the SBC network can also create an SBC account from the app ("Créer mon compte sur SBC"), and then sign in with "Se connecter avec SBC".

5. Is there any paid content in the app, and who pays for it?
The app is free and has no in-app purchases. Opening an SBC Network account is free. SBC membership is bought from SBC on its own website, outside the app, and isn't sold or promoted in the app. The app only checks whether an account has an active membership before showing the member directory.
